# CREATIVE RULES (authoritative copy is the Style / Hook sections of CLAUDE.md)
- Original English horror stories, 2D flat animation, vertical 9:16, length 50–60 s (owner 2026-11-16, per research), new beat every ≤ 6 s.
- English narration + word-by-word English captions; atmospheric SFX; quiet mystical music.
- Hook in the first 2 s: warning or impossible fact ("Never open the door if…", "If you hear this at night, don't…", "Whatever you do, don't…") — patterns, not scripts to repeat.
- Escalating suspense, cliffhanger at the end of Parts 1–2, twist in Part 3; last line loops to the first.
- 3 parts per story; Part 2/3 open with a 1-sentence recap hook.
- YouTube-safe: creepy, no gore, nothing about harm to children; no existing IP.
- Titles ≤ 60 chars: hook as promise + emoji + #shorts.

## Genres (from 2026-11-15)
Horror, mystic/supernatural, unexplained mystery, time & reality bends, eerie "documented" fiction — rotate between series.
## Rewatch mechanics (≥ 2 per episode)
Hidden early clue · loop ending · open question for comments · recontextualizing twist · occasional spotting challenge.

## First second (owner, 2026-11-15)
No greeting/intro. Line 1 = warning / "if you try this…" / "I tried / I found…" / impossible fact, ≤ 12 words, spoken by 0.4 s. Enforced by pipeline/check_hook.py.

## Genre blend (owner, 2026-11-15)
Each new series mixes 2–3 genres across its 3 parts (hook genre → different engine → genre-flip twist), one coherent logic, all clues pay off.

## Variety (owner, 2026-11-15)
Each series = a different everyday situation (elevator, highway, hotel, last train, smart speaker…), never another 2 AM/door/knock story. No repeat of the last 3 series' setting, time, monster or key prop. Header stamp fits the story.

## Pacing template from the best video (owner 2026-11-18, YouTube Studio analysis: 2 AM Knock P1 = best retention/rewatch)
"Never Open The Door At 2 AM" — 1366 views, 106 % avg viewed. Every NEW Part 1 (and as far as possible Parts 2/3) follows its beat map:
1. 0–2 s: one-sentence warning/conflict + the threat already on screen (door, phone, face) — the conflict/mystery is clear in the first 2–3 s.
2. ~2–5 s: who/where in ≤ 8 words (home alone, 9th floor) — no backstory.
3. by ~5 s: the first concrete event (knock / call / message) — never later than line 3.
4. ~8–12 s: a FAMILIAR thing turns wrong (mom at the door).
5. middle: hard evidence that contradicts it (text from the real mom in Chicago).
6. escalation: an impossible detail (she never blinks → it speaks → knocks on a 9th-floor window).
7. last line: loop back to the opening in the present tense ("It's 1:59 right now… three slow knocks").
Studio advice to follow: lift "Stayed to watch" by making the first frame + first line state the conflict instantly; spread tension evenly (a new beat every 4–5 s).

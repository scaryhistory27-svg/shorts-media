# The Missing Floor — 3-part arc (started 2026-11-15)
**Premise:** "Never step out if your elevator stops on floor 13." Office building with 12 floors; at 4:47 PM the elevator stops on 13.
**Why chosen (scores /10: hook, relatable, rule, rewatch, twist):** elevator 9/9/9/8/8 = 43 · voicemail from tomorrow 41 · extra person in
family photo 39 · hiker camera 35 · 4 AM gas station 34 (too close to the last series' night setting).
Web trends (2026): series-based shorts, liminal/analog horror, found-footage evidence, urban-legend "rules" stories.
**Differs from The 2 AM Knock:** daytime (4:47 PM) not night · office/elevator not apartment door · rule = don't step out (not "don't answer")
· creature = frozen people / a woman who only turns her head (not a spider-thing) · key prop = elevator panel + reflection (not door/phone).
**Genres:** Part 1 horror + liminal mystery → Part 2 "documented" evidence (CCTV, maintenance log) → Part 3 reality/doppelgänger twist.

- **Part 1:** elevator stops on 13, doors open to a frozen copy of his office, a woman turns only her head: "You're late." He closes the doors —
  cliffhanger: his reflection stayed on floor 13. Hidden clue: a BLANK 13th button glows for ~0.5 s on the panel (scene "panel"); in the frozen
  office one frozen worker wears HIS blue hoodie (scene "office"). Loop: end → "Never step out…".
- **Part 2:** at home his mirrors are empty; building CCTV shows his elevator never left the lobby for 26 minutes; maintenance log: "Car 2 — stops at
  13 every day 4:47. DO NOT SERVICE." Old badge photos of employees who "left at 4:47". Cliffhanger: tomorrow's 4:47 alarm set on his phone — he didn't set it.
- **Part 3:** he rides at 4:47 to get his reflection back; on 13 the frozen worker in his hoodie is HIM — the one who stepped out years ago; the
  reflection came back… so the one who goes home is the reflection. Twist recontextualizes Part 1 ("You're late" = he was expected). End: open question
  "Which one of us went home?" End card THE END?
